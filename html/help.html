Account and login

How can I delete my account?
  1. Log in and click your username in the top navigation. This opens the User Area. On a phone, open the navigation menu first.
  2. Choose Delete account in the left menu. On a phone, open the menu with the button at the top left.
  3. Type DELETE into the field and click Delete account permanently.

This cannot be undone. Your account, profile, favorites and calculation history are removed from our database. A few technical copies at our service providers expire on their own, see Privacy Policy, section 7.

Can't log in? Try Forgot password first. If that does not work, email us from the address registered to your account and ask us to delete it.

I forgot my password. What can I do?
  1. On the login page, click Forgot password?
  2. Enter the email address of your account and click Send reset link.
  3. Open the email, click the link and choose a new password (at least 8 characters).

No email? Wait a few minutes and check your spam folder. For security, we show the same message whether or not an account exists for the address, so check that you entered the address you registered with.

The link works only once and only for a limited time. If it says "invalid or expired", request a new one.

If your email address was not confirmed yet, the reset link confirms it as well.

Can I log in with my username?

No. Log in with your email address and your password. Your username is only shown to you in your account and is not visible to other users.

I did not get the confirmation email, or the link does not work
  1. Wait a few minutes and check your spam folder.
  2. On the "Verification email sent" page, click Resend email. The button is available again after 60 seconds and works only in the browser tab where you registered.
  3. If you closed that page, register again with the same email address. We send a new confirmation link. If the form says your username is already taken, your first attempt reserved it. Enter any other username: the new email is sent anyway, and your account keeps the username from your first attempt.

Confirmation links work only for a limited time. Registrations that are never confirmed are deleted automatically after 14 days.

If you already have an account with this address, you may not receive a new email. Try logging in, or use Forgot password.

If you try to log in before confirming, Globomath shows a notice with a link to send the email again. If a page says the link is "expired or already used", some email programs open links automatically, which uses them up. Request a new link in that case.

How do I change my email address, username or password?

Click your username in the top navigation to open the User Area, then:

  • Email address: in Account, click Change next to your email. Enter the new address and your current password. We send a confirmation link to your current and to your new address, and you have to click both. Until then, your current address stays active.
  • Username: in Account, click Edit. Use 3 to 20 characters: letters, numbers, _, - and .. Confirm with your current password.
  • Password: in Security, use Change password. The new password needs at least 8 characters.
Do I need an account? Who can register?

You can use all tools without an account. An account is free and lets you save your settings, favorites and the Math Calculator history, and use Advanced Mode, on all your devices.

Accounts are for users aged 14 or older. Younger students can use the tools without an account. Globomath shows no advertising and does not track you, see the Privacy Policy.

I cannot log in
  • "Email or password is incorrect": log in with your email address, not your username, and check the password. Forgot it? Use Forgot password.
  • "Please confirm your email address first": your account is not activated yet. Use the link in the message to send the confirmation email again, see confirmation email.
  • "Too many failed attempts": wait a moment. The pause lasts between 30 seconds and 5 minutes.
  • Password is correct but login still fails: your account may have been suspended. We only suspend accounts for good cause and tell you by email, see Terms of Service, section 10. Write to us if you think this is a mistake.
Why am I asked to accept the Privacy Policy or Terms of Service?

When we make significant changes to one of these documents, logged-in users are asked to accept the new version. Smaller changes are only announced, see Terms of Service, section 15. If you do not agree, you can log out and keep using the tools without an account, or delete your account in the User Area. The User Area stays reachable without accepting.

Using the tools

Why does Advanced Mode ask me to log in?

Advanced Mode (extra units, extra percentage tools and extra calculator functions) is available with an account. Log in, then use the Advanced Mode switch on the tool page. You can also switch it on and off for all tools in the User Area under Extra settings.

How do I save a tool as a favorite?

Log in, then click the heart on a tool card on the homepage, or the heart on the right side of a tool page. Your favorites appear at the top of the homepage and in the User Area under My favorites.

Where is my calculation history?

The Math Calculator keeps a history for logged-in users. Calculations made while logged out are not saved. Open History below the keypad and click an entry to use it again. Only the most recent 50 entries are kept. Delete single entries with the trash icon, or all of them with Delete history.

How do I change the design, font size or currency?

Log in and open the User Area. Under Appearance you can choose the design (Abyss, Dark or Light), the accent color and the font size, then click Save design. On phones the font size is limited to 20px. Currency and decimal places are under Extra settings.

Problems

The formula field does not appear ("Math components could not be loaded")

The formula input of the Formula Transformer, the Equation Solver and the Math Calculator is loaded from an external service. Check your internet connection and reload the page. Ad blockers and network filters, for example in a school or company network, can block it. Turn the blocker off for globomath.com, or try another network or device.

A result looks wrong

Results and steps are generated automatically and can contain errors. Some tools support only certain inputs or show only one solution, so please check important results yourself, see the Terms of Service, section 8.

If you think a tool is wrong, use Feedback in the footer, choose Report a bug and describe the input you used. Please do not include personal data.
