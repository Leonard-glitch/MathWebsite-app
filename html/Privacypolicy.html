Privacy Policy

1. Privacy at a glance

General information

The following notes give a simple overview of what happens to your personal data when you visit this website. Personal data are all data that can be used to identify you personally.

2. Data collection on our website

Cookies & local storage

This website partly uses local storage in your browser to save your favorites or settings, such as the selected bases in the calculator. This data remains locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Server log files

The provider of the pages automatically collects and stores information in so-called server log files that your browser automatically sends to us. These are:

  • Browser type and browser version
  • Operating system used
  • Referrer URL
  • Host name of the accessing computer
  • Time of the server request
  • IP address

3. Your rights regarding your data

You have the right to obtain information free of charge about the origin, recipient and purpose of your stored personal data at any time. You also have the right to request the correction or deletion of this data. If you have any questions about data protection, you can contact us at the address given in the imprint at any time.
