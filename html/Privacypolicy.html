2. Scope and principles

This policy applies to the website globomath.com and all its pages, tools, the user account, the feedback form and email contact with us.

We follow a few simple principles:

  • We only process the data we need to run Globomath securely.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools, and we do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not create profiles of you and make no automated decisions about you.

Our legal bases under the GDPR are: providing the account and features you request (Art. 6(1)(b)), our legitimate interests in a secure, stable and improvable website and in answering your requests (Art. 6(1)(f)), and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). We do not currently rely on consent. If this changes, we will ask you first.

3. Hosting, CDN and security (Cloudflare)

Globomath is hosted and delivered through Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) ("Cloudflare"). Cloudflare provides hosting, a content delivery network (CDN) and security services that protect the website against attacks and abuse. When you visit any page, your browser automatically sends technical data to Cloudflare's servers:

  • IP address, date and time of the request
  • requested URL and referring page
  • browser type and device or operating system information (user agent)
  • approximate location (country), derived from the IP address
  • technical security signals, for example whether a request looks like an attack

Purpose and legal basis: delivering the website, protecting it against attacks and misuse, and keeping it stable (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. In addition, Cloudflare may use certain technical connection data for its own network security purposes, as described in its privacy policy.

Retention: Cloudflare keeps standard logs and statistics for a limited time under our current plan [CONFIRM: exact retention period of the Cloudflare plan]. We do not use these logs to analyse individual visitors.

5. Your account: registration and login

You do not need an account to use the tools. If you create one, we process:

  • your email address, a username and your password
  • technical account data: account ID, creation date, confirmation status
  • proof of your agreement: the versions of the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service you accepted, the date and time of each acceptance (in UTC), a fingerprint (hash) of the document text of that version, and the fact that you confirmed that you are at least 14 years old
  • session data and security data from the authentication service, such as your IP address, browser type and timestamps when you sign in

How it works:

  • Registration: after you register, we send a confirmation link to your email address. The account is activated when you click it.
  • Proof of acceptance: when you tick the boxes during registration (or confirm updated documents after logging in), our server records which version you accepted and when. The versions and the time are set by our server, not by your browser. We do not store your IP address or browser details for this purpose. You can see these records in your own account data on request (section 16).
  • Username: your username is only used for your own account and is not visible to other users. When you choose a username, our database checks whether it is still available.
  • Password: your password is never stored in plain text. It is stored in securely hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • Login: you sign in with your email address and password. Your login session is kept in your browser until you log out or the session expires.
  • Password reset and email change: we send you a link by email. When you change your email address, confirmation links are sent to your current and your new address, and both must be confirmed. Until then, your current address stays active.

Purpose and legal basis: providing and securing the account you request (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR). The proof of acceptance is stored so that we can show which documents you agreed to if there is a dispute (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR: concluding the agreement on the use of your account, and our legitimate interest in establishing, exercising or defending legal claims). Authentication and the database are provided by Supabase (see section 13); the database is located in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).

Accounts are for users aged 14 or older (see section 19).

6. Data stored in your account

So that your settings follow you across devices, we store the following in your account:

  • Settings: theme, design, font size, currency, decimal places, live-result and angle mode settings, advanced-mode switches, and tool selections (for example your last chosen unit category and units)
  • Personalisation: favorite tools, pinned groups and the order of tool cards
  • Calculation history (for example in the Math Calculator): the expression, the result and the time. Only the most recent entries are kept (currently up to 50 per tool [CONFIRM: limit applies per tool]). You can delete single entries or the whole history yourself.
  • Account information: creation date and an internal status flag (currently not in use)

Guest data: if you registered after using Globomath as a guest, the settings and history stored locally in your browser are copied into your new account. The local guest history is then removed.

Legal basis: providing the features you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Access: database rules (row-level security) ensure that each user can only access their own data. The operator has technical access to the database for operation, security and support; this access is protected by two-factor authentication.

We use these logs only to detect and prevent misuse and to find technical errors (Art. 6(1)(f) and Art. 32 GDPR). They are kept for the standard retention periods of our current provider plans [CONFIRM: exact periods for Supabase logs and authentication audit data].

Further security measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, row-level security in the database, security headers in the website, two-factor authentication for all administrator accounts, and access limited to the operator.

16. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you (Art. 15)
  • Rectification of incorrect data (Art. 16)
  • Erasure of your data (Art. 17)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability: receive your data in a common, machine-readable format (Art. 20). We provide this manually on request.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (we do not currently rely on consent)

How to exercise your rights: send an email to contact@globomath.com. To protect your account, we verify your identity by checking that the request comes from the email address registered to your account. We answer within one month; in complex cases this period may be extended by up to two further months, and we will tell you. This is free of charge.

Self-service: in the User Area you can change your username, email address and password, and delete your account.
